SOMETHING FROM THE HEART

A Little Letter for Sumu 💙

Hey Sumu, 🦚🌷

There are a few things I've wanted to tell you, and I thought this little letter would be the perfect place to do that. So, take your time reading this, okay? 💙

First of all, I really hope that someday you stop calling yourself boring, because honestly, I don't see you that way at all. You don't have to be the loudest person in the room, talk all the time, or always know what to say to be interesting. Being quiet doesn't make you boring, and being shy doesn't make you any less special.

Sometimes, I wish you could see yourself the way someone else might see you. Maybe then you'd understand that the little things you think are ordinary about yourself can actually be the things that make you special. 🌷

And Sumu, there's one thing I really want you to remember whenever you're talking to me: please, just be yourself, okay?

You don't have to think a hundred times before saying something. You don't have to worry that I'll feel bad if you say something differently than I expected. You don't always have to find the perfect words, and you don't have to be afraid of being yourself around me.

If you feel like talking, talk. If you want to share something random, share it. If you want to rant about your day, I'll listen. If you want to tease me, disagree with me, or tell me I'm being stupid sometimes, go ahead. 😂💙

And if you don't feel like talking much on some days, that's okay too. You don't have to force conversations or pretend to be someone you're not just to keep things going.

I'd rather have a real conversation with you than have you worrying about every little word because you think it might hurt my feelings.

I want you to feel comfortable enough to tell me what's on your mind, even when it's something you think I might not want to hear. You can be honest with me. You don't always have to agree with me or make me happy. Your comfort matters too, and I hope you never feel that you have to hide your thoughts just to protect mine. 🫶🏻

Honestly, I'm really glad I got the chance to know you. It's funny how some people slowly become important to us without even realizing it themselves. A small conversation, a random message, or just seeing them smile can make an ordinary day feel a little better.

Maybe you don't always realize how much the little things about you can mean to someone. Maybe you don't see what makes you different from everyone else. But I hope you never underestimate yourself, Sumu. 🌻

And I hope you know that you don't have to change your quiet nature for anyone. You can be shy, silly, sleepy, talkative, quiet, random, or anything in between. You don't have to become a different person to deserve someone's time, care, or affection.

I don't know what the future holds, and I don't think every feeling needs an answer right away. But I do know that I'm happy I got to know you, and there's a part of me that always wishes to see you happy, smiling, and feeling good about yourself.

So, Sumu, for your birthday, I hope you get countless reasons to smile, beautiful memories to look back on, your favourite Taylor Swift songs playing at the perfect moments, and all the little things that make your heart happy. 🎶🌷

I hope this new year of your life helps you see yourself with a little more kindness. Be patient with yourself, celebrate your little achievements, and remember that even on the days when you don't feel your best, you are still worthy of happiness and love.

And if there's one thing I hope you remember from this little letter, it's that you never have to pretend to be someone else to be special to someone. 💙

Happy Birthday in advance, Sumu! 🎂🦚 Keep being you, keep chasing the little things that make you happy, and please don't be so hard on yourself, okay?

I hope this little surprise brings a smile to your face, because you genuinely deserve beautiful things and happy moments.

Take care of yourself, Sumu. 💙
And never forget how special it is to simply be yourself. 🌷

— Someone who's really glad you're in his life. 💗
